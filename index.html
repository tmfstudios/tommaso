Our Story

A tradition that
continues.

For generations, Tommaso has been a part of the Brooklyn dining tradition. What began at our original location at 1464 86th Street became a beloved neighborhood gathering place, where friends and families came together to enjoy classic Italian cooking, good wine, warm hospitality, and memorable evenings around the table.

After the passing of our founder and owner, Tommaso Verdillo, the restaurant’s legacy continued through the people who had worked alongside him and shared his passion for bringing people together through food. While the doors at our original location eventually closed, the spirit of Tommaso did not.

Today, we proudly welcome you to our new home at 6916 18th Avenue, where we carry forward the traditions that made Tommaso special. The recipes, the hospitality, the familiar warmth, and the love of Italian dining continue in a new chapter.

The Experience

More than a meal —
a true occasion.

At Tommaso, dining is about taking a moment to enjoy great food, attentive service, and a welcoming atmosphere. Our kitchen brings the traditions of Italian cuisine to the table through thoughtfully prepared dishes, quality ingredients, and classic flavors.

Whether you’re joining us for an intimate dinner, an evening with friends, or a special occasion, every detail is designed to make your experience comfortable and memorable. From the first course to the final glass of wine, we invite you to relax, enjoy yourself, and experience the tradition that has made Tommaso a part of Brooklyn for generations.
