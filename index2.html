Our Story

Born from a mother's
love for feeding people.

After losing his beloved mother, Thomas Verdillo opened Tommaso as a living tribute — carrying on her joy for cooking, her generosity, and her belief that a great meal is an act of love.

For over five decades, Tommaso has been the heartbeat of Bensonhurst: a place where neighbors become regulars, and regulars become family. From hand-rolled pasta to house-made mozzarella, every dish is a memory made edible.
